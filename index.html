How to play

A mystery country is shown as a silhouette. You have 6 guesses. After each guess you get the distance to the target, a direction arrow, and a proximity % (100% = correct).

The silhouette defaults to an orthographic view — the country as seen from directly overhead in space, with almost no distortion. That's its true shape. Use the toggle to redraw the same country in:

• Mercator — preserves local shape but stretches area badly toward the poles (this is what the original Worldle uses).
• Gall–Peters — preserves area but stretches shape.

No flat map can keep both shape and area true — only a globe can. Switch projections to feel the trade-off. Your results are saved on this device only (no login).
